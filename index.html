Methodology

How to read this dashboard

People in need is the principal humanitarian measure. The production data layer records the source, reporting period, publication date and retrieval date for each number. Crisis start dates and Christian-presence evidence are maintained as separate fields.

Christian presence is not inferred from religion statistics. “Unknown” means that the dashboard has not established sufficient evidence; it does not mean absence.

Humanitarian APIs can change their schemas or availability. The dashboard therefore reports connection success or failure rather than silently presenting stale data as live.

Sources: OCHA Humanitarian Programme Cycle · UNHCR · ReliefWeb · HDX. See data/crises.json for the curated crisis layer.
